Before you begin

Good questions. Straight answers.

Do I need to have lifted weights before?

No. The programme is designed around beginner barbell training. Your coach will help you learn the movements and select an appropriate starting load.

What if I'm worried about injury?

Tell your coach about relevant injuries, limitations, and concerns before training. Exercises and loads can be adapted. If you have a medical concern, seek appropriate clinical advice before starting. The programme does not replace medical care or guarantee injury prevention.

What does the DEXA scan tell me?

Depending on the scan and protocol, DEXA can estimate body composition and assess bone mineral density. It is a measurement tool, not a guarantee of future health or a substitute for medical diagnosis. Ask the programme team what the included scan covers.

Is this just another gym membership?

No. It's a defined eight-week programme with coached small-group sessions, structured progression, a baseline scan, and progress checks. Ask the team to confirm session frequency, scheduling, assessment arrangements, and everything included before booking.

Can I help a parent get started?

Yes. You can explore the programme together. The participant should choose to take part, and any personal assessment or health information should be shared with their consent.
